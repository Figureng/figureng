How to calculate fuel cost

Fuel cost depends on three main numbers: distance travelled, vehicle fuel consumption and petrol price. A vehicle rated at 10 litres per 100 km uses about 10 litres over a 100 km trip under the stated consumption assumption.

Fuel needed = Distance × Consumption ÷ 100

Fuel cost = Fuel needed × Price per litre

Example fuel calculation

If your trip is 300 km, your vehicle uses 8 litres per 100 km, and petrol costs ₦1,000 per litre, estimated fuel use is 24 litres. Estimated fuel cost is ₦24,000.

Why real fuel use can be different

  • Traffic and frequent stops can increase consumption.
  • High speed, heavy loads and air conditioning can affect fuel use.
  • Tyre pressure, vehicle condition and driving style also matter.
  • Pump prices can change, so use the price you expect to pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include a return trip?

It calculates the distance you enter. For a return trip, enter the combined outbound and return distance.

What if I do not know my vehicle's consumption?

Check the owner's manual or use a recent real-world fuel-use record. Manufacturer figures can differ from actual driving.

Does this predict exactly how much petrol I will buy?

No. It is an estimate based on your inputs. Traffic, weather, vehicle condition and driving style can change actual consumption.
