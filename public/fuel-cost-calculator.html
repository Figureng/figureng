How to calculate fuel cost in Nigeria

Fuel cost depends on the distance travelled, your vehicle's fuel consumption and the price you pay per litre. The calculator uses the consumption figure in litres per 100 kilometres.

Fuel needed = Distance × Consumption ÷ 100

Fuel cost = Fuel needed × Petrol price per litre

Example

A 300 km journey in a vehicle using 8 L/100 km requires an estimated 24 litres. At ₦1,000 per litre, the estimated fuel cost is ₦24,000.

Why actual fuel use can differ

  • Traffic and frequent stops can increase fuel consumption.
  • Speed, air conditioning and heavy loads affect fuel use.
  • Tyre pressure, vehicle condition and driving style also matter.
  • Petrol prices vary, so use the price you expect to pay.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include a return trip?

It calculates the distance you enter. For a return trip, enter the combined outbound and return distance.

What if I do not know my fuel consumption?

Check your owner's manual or use a recent real-world fuel-use record. Actual consumption can differ from manufacturer figures.

Is the result exact?

No. It is an estimate based on your inputs. Driving conditions and vehicle performance can change actual fuel use.
