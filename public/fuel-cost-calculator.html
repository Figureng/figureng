How the fuel cost calculator works

The calculator estimates the amount of petrol required from your trip distance and vehicle fuel efficiency.

Fuel required = Distance ÷ Vehicle efficiency

It then multiplies the estimated litres required by the petrol price you entered.

Fuel cost = Litres required × Petrol price

For example, a vehicle that travels 10 kilometres per litre and makes a 100-kilometre one-way trip would require about 10 litres of petrol.

Why petrol prices vary in Nigeria

There is not one permanent pump price that applies to every filling station in Nigeria.

Petrol prices can vary according to location, supply conditions, distributor pricing, logistics and movements in the downstream petroleum market.

Recent August 2026 reports demonstrate how quickly prices can change. Dangote Refinery's PMS gantry price moved from ₦1,185 to ₦1,200 per litre effective August 26, 2026.

Therefore, FigureNG does not present ₦1,200 as a permanent national pump price. Instead, the calculator allows you to enter the actual price you are paying.

Understanding vehicle fuel efficiency

Fuel efficiency describes how far a vehicle can travel using a given amount of petrol.

For example, if your vehicle travels approximately 10 kilometres using one litre of petrol, its fuel efficiency is 10 km/L.

Actual fuel consumption can change because of traffic, driving style, road conditions, vehicle condition, air conditioning, tyre pressure, load and other factors.

Tip: For a more accurate personal estimate, calculate your vehicle's real-world fuel consumption from your own recent trips rather than relying only on the manufacturer's quoted figure.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate fuel cost for a trip?

Divide the distance by your vehicle's kilometres-per-litre efficiency to estimate litres required. Then multiply the litres by the current petrol price per litre.

How much fuel do I need for 100 km?

It depends on your vehicle. A vehicle achieving 10 km/L would need approximately 10 litres for 100 km.

How much does it cost to travel 100 km by car in Nigeria?

The answer depends on the vehicle's fuel efficiency and the petrol price. At 10 km/L and ₦1,200 per litre, the estimated fuel cost is ₦12,000.

What is km/L?

km/L means kilometres per litre. It indicates approximately how many kilometres a vehicle can travel using one litre of fuel.

Does the calculator use today's Nigerian petrol price?

No. Petrol prices can vary between locations and change frequently. Enter the price you are currently paying at your filling station for a more relevant estimate.

Can I use this calculator for a return journey?

Yes. Select Return Trip and the calculator will double the entered one-way distance before calculating the estimated fuel requirement and cost.
