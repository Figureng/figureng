What is VAT in Nigeria?

Value Added Tax, usually called VAT, is a consumption tax applied to taxable goods and services. When VAT applies to a transaction, the tax may be added to the price or included in the amount already quoted.

For a standard 7.5% calculation, a taxable amount of ₦100,000 produces ₦7,500 VAT. The total after adding VAT is ₦107,500.

Example: ₦100,000 × 7.5% = ₦7,500 VAT. ₦100,000 + ₦7,500 = ₦107,500.

How to calculate 7.5% VAT

Adding VAT

Multiply the price before VAT by 0.075. Then add the VAT to the original amount.

VAT = Amount × 7.5%
Total = Amount + VAT

Removing VAT

If a price already includes 7.5% VAT, divide the VAT-inclusive amount by 1.075 to find the amount before VAT. The difference between the inclusive price and the pre-VAT amount is the VAT.

Frequently asked questions

What VAT rate does this calculator use?

It starts at 7.5%, which you can change in the calculator if a different applicable rate is required.

How do I remove VAT from a price?

For a price that already includes 7.5% VAT, divide it by 1.075. The difference is the VAT portion.
