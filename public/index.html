Free Nigerian calculators

Calculate salary, tax, loans, business costs and household expenses in Nigeria

FigureNG is a growing collection of free online calculators built for practical Nigerian needs. Instead of giving you a number with no context, our tools are designed to help you understand how the result was reached and what it can mean.

Salary and PAYE calculators

Calculate Nigerian salary, take-home pay, PAYE, pension contributions and salary increases. These tools are useful when comparing job offers, reviewing a payslip or planning your monthly income.

Loan and business calculators

Estimate loan repayments and interest, compare flat and reducing-balance calculations, and work out business profit, margin and markup before making a decision.

VAT, fuel and electricity calculators

Calculate VAT on Nigerian transactions, estimate fuel costs for a journey, calculate electricity units and estimate household electricity costs. Each tool is designed to use straightforward inputs and explain the result.
