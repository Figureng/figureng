The FigureNG approach

Useful first. Clear always.

FigureNG is being built as a practical reference point for calculations that affect everyday decisions in Nigeria. The goal is not to overwhelm you with features. It is to give you the number you need and explain where it came from.

01
Built for Nigerian users Currency, terminology and local context are considered where they apply.
02
Transparent methodology Important calculations include explanations and assumptions.
03
No unnecessary barriers Basic tools are designed to work without requiring an account.
04
Designed for real screens The experience is built to work properly on phones as well as larger displays.
