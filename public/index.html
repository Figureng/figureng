What is this calculator?

FigureNG helps you estimate what may remain from your gross salary after applicable deductions and PAYE tax.

The calculator uses the 2026 Nigerian personal income tax structure and allows users to enter eligible deductions that may reduce chargeable income.

Because individual circumstances differ, the result is an estimate and should not be treated as an official tax assessment.
