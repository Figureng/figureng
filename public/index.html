What is the Nigeria Salary Calculator?

FigureNG's salary calculator helps Nigerian employees estimate how much of their gross salary they may receive after applicable deductions.

Your gross salary is not always the same as the amount that reaches your bank account. Depending on your circumstances, deductions may include PAYE tax, pension contributions and other applicable items.

This calculator provides an estimate using the assumptions built into the tool. It is intended as a convenient planning tool rather than a replacement for an official tax calculation.

What does the calculation include?

The result is presented as a breakdown rather than one unexplained number.

Gross income

Your annual salary before the deductions included by the calculator.

PAYE tax

An estimated annual and monthly PAYE amount based on the calculator's tax-band assumptions.

Pension

The calculator displays the pension contribution used by its current calculation rules.

NHF

Where applicable, the calculator can account for the NHF deduction used by its current rules.

Rent relief

The calculator considers the annual rent entered and applies the rent-relief assumption built into the current calculation.

Important information This calculator provides an estimate. Tax rules, thresholds, pension requirements, rent-relief rules and other regulations can change. Always verify important financial or tax information with the appropriate Nigerian authority or a qualified professional.
