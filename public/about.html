About FigureNG

Simple, useful digital tools built with Nigerians in mind.

What is FigureNG?

FigureNG is a growing collection of free online tools, calculators and practical resources designed to help people make everyday decisions more easily.

We focus on information and tools that are useful in everyday Nigerian life, from salary calculations and personal finance to other practical questions people regularly search for online.

Our goal is simple: make useful information easier to understand and useful tools easier to access.

What we create

FigureNG is being developed around several categories, including:

  • Nigerian calculators
  • Personal finance tools
  • Salary and income resources
  • Everyday decision-making tools
  • Practical Nigerian information

Our approach

We aim to make every tool simple enough for an ordinary person to use without technical knowledge.

Where calculations are provided, we explain how they work instead of simply presenting a number.

We also aim to clearly identify estimates, assumptions and limitations so visitors can understand what a result means.

Accuracy matters

We make reasonable efforts to keep information and calculations up to date. However, laws, regulations, prices and other information can change.

Therefore, information on FigureNG should not automatically be treated as professional legal, financial, tax, medical or other specialist advice.

When an official source is relevant, visitors should verify important information with the appropriate authority or qualified professional.

Built for the web

FigureNG is designed to work well on phones, tablets and computers. Our goal is to keep the experience fast, straightforward and accessible.

We are continuously adding new tools and improving existing ones based on usefulness and visitor needs.

Contact FigureNG

If you have a question, notice an error, or have a suggestion for a useful tool, we'd like to hear from you.
