How to calculate a salary increase

A salary increase shows how much more you will earn after your employer raises your pay. The easiest way to calculate it is to multiply your current salary by the increase percentage.

Salary increase formula

Salary increase = Current salary × Increase percentage ÷ 100

Your new salary is then your current salary plus the increase amount.

Example of a 20% salary increase

If your current monthly gross salary is ₦250,000 and your employer gives you a 20% increase, the increase is ₦50,000 per month.

Your new monthly gross salary would therefore be ₦300,000.

Over 12 months, the gross increase would be ₦600,000 before considering taxes and other deductions.

Will a salary increase increase your PAYE?

It can. Nigeria uses a progressive personal income tax system. Under the 2026 tax framework, different portions of taxable income are taxed at different rates.

The first ₦800,000 of taxable income is taxed at 0%. The next ₦2.2 million is taxed at 15%, followed by higher bands of 18%, 21%, 23% and 25%.

This means moving into a higher tax band does not mean your entire salary is suddenly taxed at that higher rate. Only the portion falling within that band is taxed at that rate.

Why your take-home pay may not rise by the same percentage

A 20% increase in gross salary does not necessarily produce a 20% increase in take-home pay.

Taxes and other eligible deductions can affect the amount that reaches your bank account. This is why FigureNG shows the estimated PAYE impact separately from the gross salary increase.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a 10% salary increase?

Multiply your current salary by 10%, then add the result to your current salary. For example, a ₦200,000 salary would increase by ₦20,000 to ₦220,000.

How do I calculate a 20% salary increase?

Multiply your current salary by 0.20. Add the result to your current salary to get your new gross salary.

How much is a 30% increase on ₦300,000?

A 30% increase on ₦300,000 is ₦90,000. The new monthly gross salary would be ₦390,000.

Does a salary increase affect PAYE in Nigeria?

It can. PAYE is based on taxable income and Nigeria uses progressive personal income tax bands. The actual PAYE effect depends on your taxable income and eligible deductions.
