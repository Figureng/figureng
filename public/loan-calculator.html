How a loan repayment is calculated

This calculator uses the reducing-balance method for a standard amortising loan. Your interest is calculated on the outstanding balance, so the interest portion normally falls as the balance is paid down.

Monthly payment = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments.

Example of a ₦1 million loan

At 20% annual interest over 12 monthly payments, the calculator estimates the regular monthly payment using the reducing-balance formula. Your lender may use a different rate, fee structure or repayment method.

What to check before taking a loan

  • Compare the total repayment, not just the advertised monthly payment.
  • Check whether the interest is flat, reducing or otherwise calculated.
  • Ask about processing fees, insurance, late fees and other charges.
  • Confirm the exact repayment dates and consequences of missed payments.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator include lender fees?

No. It estimates principal and interest from the figures you enter. Add lender fees separately when comparing offers.

What is a reducing-balance loan?

Interest is calculated against the outstanding principal. As the principal falls, the interest portion can also fall.

Can I use this for any Nigerian lender?

You can use it as an estimate, but each lender can have its own rates, fees and repayment rules.
