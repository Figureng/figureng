How this Nigeria loan calculator works

This calculator estimates an amortising loan where interest is charged against the outstanding balance. The payment is calculated from the principal, annual interest rate and repayment term.

Monthly payment = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments.

Why total repayment matters

A low monthly payment does not necessarily mean a cheap loan. Compare total interest and total repayment, and ask the lender about processing fees, insurance, late charges and other costs.

Reducing-balance loans

With a reducing balance, interest is calculated on the outstanding principal. As you repay the principal, the balance falls and the interest portion generally falls as well.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include lender fees?

No. It estimates principal and interest only. Add any lender fees separately when comparing offers.

Can I use this for Nigerian banks and fintech lenders?

Yes, as an estimate. Actual lenders may use different rates, fees, schedules or interest methods.

What happens if the interest rate is zero?

The calculator simply divides the principal across the selected repayment periods.
