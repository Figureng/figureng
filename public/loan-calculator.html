Why the true cost of a loan matters

The interest rate is only one part of what a borrower may pay.

A loan can have a stated interest rate plus processing, management, insurance or other charges.

For example, if a lender approves ₦1,000,000 but deducts fees before giving you the money, you may receive less than ₦1,000,000 while still being responsible for repaying the loan according to its terms.

That is why FigureNG shows both the stated loan amount and the estimated amount you actually receive.

Frequently asked questions

What is a processing fee?

A processing or management fee is a charge that a lender may apply in addition to interest. The actual fee depends on the lender and loan agreement.

What does "deducted from loan amount" mean?

It means the fee is taken from the approved loan before the money is paid to you. For example, a ₦1,000,000 loan with ₦20,000 deducted in fees would result in an estimated cash receipt of ₦980,000.

What does "added to repayment" mean?

It means the fee is treated as an additional amount you must pay rather than being deducted from the money you receive.

What is reducing-balance interest?

Interest is calculated using the outstanding principal. As principal is repaid, the balance generally falls.

What is flat-rate interest?

Flat-rate interest is calculated using the original loan amount for the stated period rather than reducing the interest base as principal is repaid.

Does the calculator include every lender charge?

No. Only the fees you enter are included. Some lenders may have additional charges such as insurance, documentation or late-payment fees.

Is FigureNG a loan company?

No. FigureNG is an independent calculator and information website. It does not offer or approve loans.
