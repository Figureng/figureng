How to calculate electricity cost

Electricity energy cost can be estimated by multiplying the number of kilowatt-hours used by the tariff per kilowatt-hour.

Energy cost = Units used (kWh) × Tariff (₦/kWh)

For example, 100 kWh at ₦100 per kWh gives an estimated energy cost of ₦10,000.

What does kWh mean?

A kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy. It represents the amount of energy used by a 1-kilowatt appliance running for one hour, or an equivalent amount of energy used over a different period.

Why your actual bill may differ

  • Your electricity bill may contain charges other than the energy charge.
  • Your applicable tariff can depend on the customer category and tariff band.
  • Meter readings, billing periods and adjustments can affect the amount payable.
  • Always use the tariff and billing information supplied by your electricity provider for an actual payment.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate my exact electricity bill?

No. It estimates the energy charge from units and tariff. Your provider may apply other charges or adjustments.

What tariff should I enter?

Enter the applicable rate shown on your electricity bill, vending receipt or other current information from your provider.

Can I use this for prepaid electricity?

Yes. Enter the number of units purchased or consumed and the applicable energy tariff to estimate the energy cost.
