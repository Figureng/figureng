How the Nigeria Salary Calculator works

This calculator estimates your take-home salary by starting with your gross employment income and applying the relevant deductions and progressive personal income tax calculation.

Your monthly salary and allowances are converted into an annual figure. The calculator then estimates PAYE using the progressive tax bands and considers the deductions entered by the user.

The result is an estimate and should not be treated as an official tax assessment.

Gross salary vs net salary

Gross salary is the amount you earn before applicable deductions. Net salary, also called take-home pay, is the amount remaining after deductions such as PAYE and applicable contributions.

Two people with the same gross salary can have different take-home pay if their pension, rent relief, benefits or other applicable deductions are different.

2026 Nigeria PAYE tax bands

The 2026 calculation uses a progressive personal income tax structure. This means different portions of taxable income can be taxed at different rates.

  • First ₦800,000: 0%
  • Next ₦2,200,000: 15%
  • Next ₦9,000,000: 18%
  • Next ₦13,000,000: 21%
  • Next ₦25,000,000: 23%
  • Amount above ₦50,000,000: 25%

These are marginal bands. The highest rate does not automatically apply to your entire income.
