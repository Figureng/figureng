How the Nigeria Salary Calculator works

The FigureNG Nigeria Salary Calculator estimates your take-home salary by starting with your employment income, applying eligible deductions and reliefs entered into the calculator, and then applying the progressive personal income tax rates.

Nigeria's 2026 personal income tax system uses marginal tax bands. This means that your entire income is not automatically taxed at the highest rate you reach.

The Nigeria Revenue Service is the official tax authority. This calculator is an independent estimation tool and is not an official NRS assessment.

2026 Nigeria PAYE tax bands

The current annual personal income tax bands used by this calculator are:

  • First ₦800,000: 0%
  • Next ₦2,200,000: 15%
  • Next ₦9,000,000: 18%
  • Next ₦13,000,000: 21%
  • Next ₦25,000,000: 23%
  • Above ₦50,000,000: 25%

These are marginal rates. Each portion of taxable income is assessed within the applicable band.

What is rent relief?

Under the 2026 framework, the consolidated relief allowance was replaced by a capped rent relief for eligible taxpayers.

The relief is the lower of ₦500,000 or 20% of annual rent paid, subject to the applicable requirements.

For example, if eligible annual rent is ₦1,200,000, 20% is ₦240,000. If annual rent is ₦3,000,000, 20% is ₦600,000, but the relief is capped at ₦500,000.

Gross salary vs take-home salary

Gross salary is your employment income before applicable deductions. Take-home salary is what remains after applicable PAYE and deductions have been taken into account.

Your basic salary alone may not represent your total taxable employment income. Taxable allowances, bonuses and certain employment benefits can also affect the calculation.

Frequently asked questions

Is the FigureNG salary calculator free?

Yes. The calculator is free to use.

Does it use the 2026 Nigerian tax bands?

Yes. The calculator uses the 2026 progressive personal income tax bands.

Is PAYE the same as my total deductions?

No. PAYE is the income tax deducted from employment income. Other deductions, such as applicable pension or NHF contributions, can affect your take-home pay.

Can rent reduce my taxable income?

Eligible taxpayers may qualify for the statutory rent relief, subject to the applicable requirements and documentation.

Is the result an official tax calculation?

No. It is an estimate. Your employer and relevant tax authority may determine your actual tax liability based on your circumstances.

Does FigureNG store my salary information?

No. This calculator performs the calculation in your browser and does not require you to create an account.
