How to calculate electricity units in Nigeria

Electricity consumption is measured in kilowatt-hours, commonly called units. To estimate an appliance's units, convert its power from watts to kilowatts and multiply by the hours it runs.

Electricity unit formula

Units (kWh) = Power (W) ÷ 1,000 × Hours Used

For a monthly estimate, multiply the daily consumption by the number of days.

Example

A 1,000-watt appliance used for 5 hours each day uses about 5 kWh per day. Over 30 days, that is about 150 kWh.

Why calculate electricity consumption?

Knowing how many units an appliance uses can help you understand your electricity usage, compare appliances and plan your household energy budget.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is one electricity unit?

One unit is commonly equal to one kilowatt-hour, or 1 kWh.

How do I convert watts to units?

Divide watts by 1,000 to get kilowatts, then multiply by hours of use.

Does this calculate my exact electricity bill?

No. It estimates energy consumption. Your bill depends on your applicable tariff and other charges.
