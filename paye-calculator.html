PAYE Calculator Nigeria: How to Calculate PAYE in 2026

PAYE means Pay As You Earn. It is the system through which tax on employment income is calculated and remitted from an employee's salary. FigureNG's PAYE calculator is designed to help Nigerian employees estimate their personal income tax before looking at their payslip or payroll calculation.

How the 2026 Nigerian PAYE calculation works

The current Nigerian personal income tax system uses progressive annual taxable-income bands. The first ₦800,000 of taxable income is taxed at 0%. The next ₦2.2 million is taxed at 15%, followed by 18%, 21%, 23% and a 25% top rate on income above ₦50 million. citeturn0search0turn0search3

What deductions are included?

This calculator lets you enter pension contributions and annual rent. Under the current rules, the former consolidated relief allowance has been replaced by rent relief of the lower of ₦500,000 or 20% of annual rent, subject to the applicable conditions. Pension contributions can also qualify as a deduction. citeturn0search1turn0search9

Example

Suppose an employee has ₦6,000,000 annual gross income, ₦480,000 pension contributions and ₦1,200,000 annual rent. The rent relief used in the estimate is ₦240,000 because 20% of ₦1.2 million is below the ₦500,000 cap. The calculator then applies the progressive rates to the resulting taxable income.

PAYE and monthly salary

Although PAYE is normally withheld through payroll each month, the personal income tax bands are annual. This calculator therefore calculates annual tax first and divides the estimated amount by 12 to provide an indicative monthly PAYE figure. PAYE is generally remitted by employers by the 10th day of the following month. citeturn0search2

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PAYE in Nigeria?

PAYE is Pay As You Earn, the employment-income tax collection system used for employees.

What is the PAYE tax rate in Nigeria in 2026?

Nigeria uses progressive PIT rates from 0% on the first ₦800,000 of taxable income to a top marginal rate of 25% above ₦50 million. citeturn0search0

Is the first ₦800,000 tax free?

Under the current PIT bands, the first ₦800,000 of taxable income is taxed at 0%.

How much rent relief can I claim?

The current rent relief is the lower of ₦500,000 or 20% of annual rent, subject to the applicable requirements. citeturn0search9

Does this calculator replace my employer's payroll calculation?

No. It is an estimation tool. Your employer's payroll records and the relevant tax authority determine the actual amount payable.
