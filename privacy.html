Privacy Policy

How FigureNG handles information when you use our website.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FigureNG. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be collected, used and shared when you visit our website.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be processed when you use FigureNG and how that information may be used.

By using FigureNG, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information we collect

FigureNG is designed so that many of our tools can be used without creating an account or submitting personal information.

Depending on how you interact with the website, information may include:

  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.
  • Technical information automatically provided by your browser or device.
  • General usage information, such as pages visited, approximate location and browser type, where analytics or advertising services are enabled.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through our calculators.

3. Calculator information

Some FigureNG calculators perform calculations directly in your browser.

Where a calculator is specifically described as operating locally in your browser, the values entered into that calculator are not intentionally transmitted to FigureNG simply for the purpose of performing the calculation.

Do not enter passwords, bank details, card numbers, identification numbers or other sensitive information into any FigureNG tool unless the tool explicitly requests it for a clearly stated purpose.

4. Contact information

If you contact FigureNG by email, we may receive the information contained in your message, including your email address and any information you choose to provide.

We may use this information to respond to your message, investigate reported errors, improve our services or address other legitimate requests.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

FigureNG may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to improve website functionality and, where applicable, understand how visitors use the site.

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored by your browser. They can help websites remember preferences and provide certain functionality.

You can usually control or disable cookies through the settings of your web browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

6. Advertising

FigureNG may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to help deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance and provide advertisements that may be relevant to visitors.

Where Google advertising services are used, Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and advertising technologies.

FigureNG does not control the information practices of independent advertising providers.

7. Analytics

FigureNG may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website.

Analytics information can help us understand which pages are useful, identify technical problems and improve the website.

Analytics providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Third-party services

FigureNG may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising, security, communications and other website functions.

These providers may process technical or other information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Examples may include website hosting and infrastructure providers, analytics providers and advertising networks.

9. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

FigureNG is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

11. Your choices

Depending on your browser, device and location, you may have options relating to cookies, advertising and certain forms of data processing.

You can also contact us if you have a privacy-related question or concern.

12. External links

FigureNG may contain links to websites operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology, legal requirements or other circumstances.

The latest version will be published on this page.

14. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact FigureNG through our Contact page .
