How to calculate electricity cost in Nigeria

The basic energy charge can be estimated by multiplying the electricity units used by the tariff per kilowatt-hour.

Energy cost = Units used (kWh) × Tariff (₦/kWh)

For example, 100 kWh at ₦100 per kWh gives an estimated energy cost of ₦10,000.

What is a kilowatt-hour?

A kilowatt-hour, or kWh, is a unit of energy. It is commonly used to measure electricity consumption on meters and bills.

Why your actual bill may differ

  • Your bill may include charges beyond the basic energy charge.
  • Applicable tariffs can depend on customer category and tariff band.
  • Billing periods, meter readings and adjustments can affect the amount payable.
  • Use the tariff shown in your current electricity information for an actual payment.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate my exact electricity bill?

No. It estimates the energy charge from units and tariff. Your provider may apply other charges or adjustments.

What tariff should I enter?

Enter the applicable rate shown on your current electricity bill, vending receipt or provider information.

Can I use this for prepaid electricity?

Yes. Enter the units purchased or consumed and the applicable tariff to estimate the energy cost.
