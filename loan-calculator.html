What is a loan calculator?

A loan calculator helps you estimate how much you may need to repay when borrowing money.

Enter the amount you want to borrow, the annual interest rate and the length of the loan. FigureNG then estimates your monthly repayment and the total interest payable.

This can help you compare different borrowing scenarios before making a financial decision.

How the calculation works

This calculator uses the standard reducing-balance amortization formula.

The monthly interest rate is calculated from the annual interest rate. The calculator then estimates the fixed monthly payment required to repay the loan over the selected period.

Monthly Payment = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

Where P is the principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments.

Important This calculator provides an estimate only. Actual loan repayments may differ because lenders can use different interest calculations, fees, insurance charges, taxes, penalties and other terms. Always check the lender's official repayment schedule before accepting a loan.

Frequently asked questions

How is monthly loan repayment calculated?

For a reducing-balance loan, the calculator uses the loan amount, monthly interest rate and number of repayment periods to estimate a fixed monthly payment.

Does a longer loan duration reduce monthly repayment?

Usually, yes. Spreading the loan over more months can reduce the monthly payment, but it can also increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Does this calculator include lender fees?

No. The basic calculator estimates principal and interest. Processing fees, insurance, taxes and other lender charges may increase the actual cost of borrowing.

Can I use this calculator for Nigerian loans?

Yes. You can enter the loan amount and annual interest rate quoted by a Nigerian lender to estimate the repayment. However, always confirm whether the lender uses reducing-balance or flat-rate interest before relying on the result.
