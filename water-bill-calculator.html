How to calculate a water bill in Nigeria

A water bill normally depends on the volume of water consumed, the applicable tariff and any fixed or service charges. The exact structure varies by water provider and location.

If you have a water meter

Take the current meter reading and subtract the previous reading to find the water consumed during the billing period. Water providers may express consumption in cubic metres (m³). One cubic metre equals 1,000 litres.

Estimated bill = Water used (m³) × Tariff (₦/m³) + Fixed charges

If you are on a flat-rate bill

Some customers may be billed using a fixed monthly amount rather than a measured consumption charge. Enter the monthly amount shown on your bill.

Why you should not use one Nigerian water tariff everywhere

There is no single nationwide household water price that can accurately represent every customer. How to get a better estimate

  1. Use the consumption shown on your latest bill or meter.
  2. Use the tariff for your specific state and customer category.
  3. Add any fixed monthly charge shown by your provider.
  4. Do not treat a FigureNG estimate as an official utility invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 1 m³ of water?

One cubic metre is 1,000 litres of water.

Can I calculate my water bill without a meter?

Yes. If your provider uses a flat charge, select the flat-rate option. If you want to estimate consumption, first estimate how many litres or cubic metres your household uses.

Is the water tariff the same across Nigeria?

No. Water services and tariffs can differ by state, provider and customer category.

Does this tool give my official water bill?

No. It provides an estimate using the figures you enter.
