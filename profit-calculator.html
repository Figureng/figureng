How to calculate profit in Nigeria

Profit is the amount left after the cost of a product or service is deducted from its selling revenue. If you buy an item for ₦10,000 and sell it for ₦15,000, your gross profit is ₦5,000 before other business expenses.

Profit formula

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

For multiple units, multiply the profit per unit by the quantity sold.

What is profit margin?

Profit margin shows profit as a percentage of revenue. A ₦5,000 profit on ₦15,000 revenue gives a 33.33% profit margin.

What is markup?

Markup compares profit with the cost price. A ₦5,000 profit on a ₦10,000 cost is a 50% markup.

Why use a profit calculator?

A profit calculator helps business owners check pricing decisions quickly. You can test different selling prices before buying stock or setting a new price for customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is profit margin the same as markup?

No. Margin uses selling revenue as the base, while markup uses cost as the base.

Can I calculate profit for many products?

Yes. Enter the cost and selling price per unit, then enter the quantity sold.

Does this calculate net profit?

The main result is gross trading profit based on cost and selling price. Other operating expenses should be deducted separately for net profit.

Can I use this for a small business in Nigeria?

Yes. It can be used for retail, wholesale, food, services and other businesses where you know the relevant cost and selling price.
