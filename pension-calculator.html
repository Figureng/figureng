Pension Calculator Nigeria: How Pension Contributions Work

A pension calculator helps employees estimate how much money is being contributed toward retirement each month. For many workers in Nigeria's contributory pension system, the contribution is split between the employee and employer.

How to use the FigureNG pension calculator

  1. Enter your monthly pensionable emolument.
  2. Enter the employee contribution rate. The default is 8%.
  3. Enter the employer contribution rate. The default is 10%.
  4. Select Calculate pension contribution to see monthly and annual estimates.

What is a pension contribution?

A pension contribution is money set aside for retirement and credited to a worker's retirement savings arrangement. The amount depends on the applicable pension rules and the pensionable earnings used by the employer.

Employee and employer pension contributions

The default rates in this calculator are 8% for the employee and 10% for the employer, giving a combined contribution of 18%. These defaults are provided for estimation and should not be treated as a substitute for your employer's payroll records or official pension guidance.

Why your pensionable emolument matters

Your gross salary and your pensionable emolument are not always identical. The amount used for pension calculations depends on the applicable rules and the components of your remuneration. If you know the pensionable amount shown on your payslip, use that figure for a better estimate.

Example calculation

If your monthly pensionable emolument is ₦250,000, an 8% employee contribution is ₦20,000 per month. A 10% employer contribution is ₦25,000. The combined estimated contribution is therefore ₦45,000 per month, or ₦540,000 over 12 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the employee pension contribution in Nigeria?

The default employee rate used here is 8%. Always confirm the rate applicable to your employment and pension arrangement.

How much does an employer contribute to pension?

This calculator uses 10% as the default employer contribution for an estimate.

Does this calculator show my exact pension balance?

No. It estimates contributions. Your actual RSA balance also depends on previous contributions, investment returns, fees and other transactions.

Can I change the contribution rates?

Yes. The rate fields are editable so you can model a different approved contribution arrangement.
