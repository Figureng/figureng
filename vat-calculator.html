What is VAT in Nigeria?

Value Added Tax is a consumption tax applied to taxable goods and services. When VAT applies, it may be added to a price or already included in the amount quoted.

At 7.5%, an amount of ₦100,000 produces ₦7,500 VAT and a total of ₦107,500 when VAT is added.

Example: ₦100,000 × 7.5% = ₦7,500 VAT. ₦100,000 + ₦7,500 = ₦107,500.

How to calculate VAT

To add VAT, multiply the amount by the VAT rate and add the result to the original amount. To remove VAT from an inclusive price, divide the inclusive amount by 1 plus the VAT rate expressed as a decimal.

Frequently asked questions

What VAT rate does this calculator use?

It starts at 7.5%. You can change the rate when your calculation requires another applicable rate.

How do I remove VAT from a price?

For a 7.5% VAT-inclusive amount, divide the amount by 1.075. The difference is the VAT portion.

Can I use this calculator for large amounts?

Yes. The calculation runs in your browser and supports ordinary business and personal amounts.
